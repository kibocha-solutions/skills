# Revision Examples

## Fiction

Before: Mara closed the ledger. Her chest tightened with a sense of dread. She realized that trust, once broken, could never be repaired.

After: Mara closed the ledger. Trust, she thought, was gone.

The revision brings the reflection closer to Mara's viewpoint. A different story may call for the narrator to explain the theme directly.

## Factual writing

Before: The service restarted at 09:10. This event marked a pivotal turning point, highlighting the importance of operational resilience.

After: The service restarted at 09:10.

The revision removes unsupported commentary and retains the reported fact. Keep necessary explanations and qualifications in factual work.
