# Authority Register Guide

## Register table

| Dimension | Standard | Formal | Sovereign | Archaic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obligation | must, will | shall | shall | shall, doth |
| Permission | may | may | sovereign grant | archaic sovereign grant |
| Latin | universal terms of art | established domain terms | precise classical terms | classical and rare terms |
| Conflict opener | direct conflict rule | notwithstanding for identified conflict | notwithstanding | extended classical opener |
| Sentence form | short | moderate | classical subordination | ornate subordination |
| Voice | active | institutional | sovereign declarative | historical sovereign |
| Severability | ordinary | ordinary | sovereign-core | sovereign-core, archaic |
| Finality | modern | modern or restrained classical | classical | classical and archaic |

## Standard

Use:

- must
- will
- may
- agrees to
- is entitled to
- does not apply
- is void and unenforceable
- has no legal effect
- remains in effect
- subject to
- in accordance with

Do not use:

- archaic verb forms
- sovereign finality
- unexplained Latin
- nested clauses deeper than two levels
- `shall` unless a controlling convention requires it

Structure:

1. Use short sentences.
2. Use direct conditions and consequences.
3. Define necessary terms.
4. Use plain-language cross-references.

## Formal

Use:

- shall for obligations
- may for permissions
- must for conditions precedent
- hereby
- provided that
- subject to
- pursuant to
- identified notwithstanding clauses
- null and void
- of no force or effect

Do not use:

- archaic verb forms
- facial-death metaphors
- sovereign claims beyond the parties' lawful authority

Structure:

1. Use recitals only for context.
2. Capitalize defined terms consistently.
3. Use exact section references.
4. Limit nesting to three levels.
5. Put supplementary detail in schedules or annexes.

## Sovereign

Use:

- shall
- notwithstanding
- subsists
- repugnancy
- void ab initio
- in perpetuity
- inviolate
- sovereign grants
- preclusion clauses
- sovereign-core severability

Do not use:

- archaic verb conjugations
- decorative archaism
- clauses that require a specialist gloss to identify the command

Structure:

1. State supremacy and preclusion directly.
2. Draft trigger, scope, consequence, and survival.
3. Use exact Article, Section, and Clause references.
4. Keep one operative vector in each clause.
5. Apply the termination phrase library.

## Archaic

Use only when requested.

Permitted vocabulary includes:

- doth
- holden
- whatsoever
- whereby
- aforesaid
- hereinbefore
- hereinafter
- forasmuch as
- inasmuch as

Structure:

1. Use historical syntax without obscuring the operative command.
2. Keep one operative vector in each clause.
3. Preserve exact legal effects.
4. Keep the text parseable to a reader of classical legal English.
5. Remove fabricated or nonsensical archaism.

## Transition examples

### Severability

Standard:

> If any part of this Agreement is unenforceable, that part will not apply. The rest of the Agreement remains in effect.

Formal:

> If a court of competent jurisdiction holds any provision invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be severed. The remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

Sovereign:

> Should any provision of this Instrument be declared void ab initio by a court of competent jurisdiction, that provision shall be severed without prejudice to the remaining provisions, which shall continue to subsist in full force.

Archaic:

> Should any provision herein be adjudged repugnant to superior law, the same shall be severed and holden for naught, and the remaining provisions shall continue in force.

### Grant

Standard:

> Each Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty days' written notice.

Formal:

> Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon not fewer than thirty days' written notice delivered under Section 12.

Sovereign:

> The right to terminate this Instrument is hereby vested in each Party and is exercisable upon delivery of not fewer than thirty days' written notice under Article XII.

Archaic:

> The right and authority to bring this Instrument to its conclusion is hereby granted and vested in each Party hereto, exercisable upon the giving of not fewer than thirty days' written notice in the manner hereinbefore prescribed.
