# Termination Phrase Library

## Selection rules

1. Identify the legal effect: prospective rejection, partial severance, total invalidity, or retroactive nullity.
2. Select a phrase at the active register.
3. Use the phrase once in the instrument.
4. Do not use its functional equivalent elsewhere in the instrument.
5. Keep separate phrases only when each carries a separate legal vector.

## Phrase table

| Phrase | Register | Instrument class | Legal effect | Use | Do not use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `void ab initio` | Sovereign, Archaic | Legislative | Total retroactive nullity | Foundational repugnancy or supremacy clause | Prospective or partial invalidity |
| `shall not stand` | Sovereign, Archaic | Legislative | Prospective rejection and non-enforcement | Prohibited recognition or enforcement | Retroactive nullity |
| `shall be excised` | Sovereign, Archaic | Legislative | Total removal from the legal order | Entrenchment or unamendability | Partial invalidity |
| `null from inception` | Sovereign, Archaic | Legislative | No valid legal existence from formation | Fundamentally defective formation | Prospective invalidity |
| `stillborn upon its face` | Sovereign, Archaic | Legislative | Patent facial invalidity | Extreme facial defect at Archaic register | Ordinary invalidity |
| `will be void and unenforceable` | Standard, Formal | Prescriptive | Invalidity plus non-enforcement | Primary modern invalidity clause | A distinct retroactive effect |
| `will have no legal effect` | Standard, Formal | Prescriptive | No legal consequence | Defective waiver, consent, or modification | Required retroactive fiction |
| `is null and void` | Standard, Formal | Prescriptive, Declaratory | Traditional total invalidity | Commercial breach or termination | Duplicate modern finality |
| `shall be of no force or effect` | Formal | Legislative, Prescriptive | Instrument-imposed invalidity | Institutional conflict clause | Plain-language Standard drafting |
| `is deemed never to have taken effect` | Formal | Prescriptive | Retroactive legal fiction | Failed condition, option, or approval | Actual historical nonexistence |

## Distribution check

- [ ] Each phrase has one legal vector.
- [ ] Each phrase appears once.
- [ ] No equivalent phrase duplicates it.
- [ ] Register and legal effect match.
- [ ] Partial and total invalidity remain distinct.
- [ ] Prospective and retroactive effects remain distinct.
