# Register Calibration Examples

## Procedure

1. Select the legal concept.
2. Compare the same concept across all four registers.
3. Match vocabulary, sentence form, modal verbs, and finality to the selected register.
4. Preserve the intended legal effect.
5. Do not copy Archaic language into a lower register.

## Repugnancy

### Standard

> If any law, regulation, or rule conflicts with this Constitution, it does not apply to the extent of the conflict. The remainder remains in effect.

### Formal

> To the extent that any law, regulation, or other instrument conflicts with this Constitution, that instrument shall be of no force or effect with respect to the inconsistent provisions. Its remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

### Sovereign

> Notwithstanding any custom or decree to the contrary, where any instrument subsists in repugnancy to this Constitution, that instrument shall perish to the extent of its inconsistency and be rendered void ab initio.

### Archaic

> Whatsoever statute, decree, writ, or instrument doth subsist in repugnancy to this Constitution, the same is hereby declared holden for naught and utterly void, as though it had never been enacted, proclaimed, or conceived; and no power, authority, or instrument shall avail to restore it, notwithstanding any custom, usage, or prior enactment to the contrary.

## Entrenchment

### Standard

> This Article may not be amended, repealed, or modified. No person or body has authority to change it.

### Formal

> This Article shall not be subject to amendment, repeal, or modification by any person, body, or instrument. Any purported amendment is null and void.

### Sovereign

> This Article is the sole unamendable provision of this Constitution and shall subsist in perpetuity, inviolate, beyond the reach of any amending power, instrument, or decree.

### Archaic

> This Article is the sole unamendable provision of this Constitution and shall subsist in perpetuity, inviolate and beyond the reach of any amending power, instrument, decree, or resolution whatsoever. Any instrument, howsoever enacted, that doth purport to amend, abridge, or abrogate this Article is hereby declared null from inception and stillborn upon its face.

## Scoped nullification

### Standard

> If an instrument conflicts with this Article or is reasonably likely to produce a prohibited outcome, the conflicting parts do not apply. The remaining parts remain in effect.

### Formal

> Where a court of competent jurisdiction determines that an instrument is inconsistent with this Article or reasonably likely to produce a prohibited outcome, the inconsistent provisions shall be of no force or effect. The remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

### Sovereign

> Where an instrument produces, or is reasonably likely to produce, an outcome that this Article finds intolerable, the instrument responsible shall be nullified to the extent its operation is repugnant to this Article, and no further.

### Archaic

> Where any instrument doth produce, or is reasonably to be expected to produce, an outcome that this Article holdeth intolerable, the instrument responsible shall be nullified to the full extent of its repugnancy to this Article, and no further; and no court, tribunal, or authority shall give force to the offending provisions.

## Prohibition on recognition

### Standard

> No person may recognise, enforce, or give effect to any outcome not expressly listed in this Article. Any attempt has no legal effect.

### Formal

> No person shall recognise, declare, enforce, or give effect to any outcome not expressly enumerated within this Article. Any purported recognition or enforcement shall be of no force or effect.

### Sovereign

> No person shall recognise, declare, enforce, or give effect to any intolerable outcome not expressly enumerated within this Article; and any purported recognition shall not stand.

### Archaic

> No person, body, court, or authority whatsoever shall recognise, declare, enforce, or give effect to any intolerable outcome not expressly enumerated within this Article; and any purported recognition, declaration, or enforcement shall be holden for naught as though it had never been attempted.

## Exclusive jurisdiction

### Standard

> A court may invalidate an instrument under this Article only through Clause 4. No other legal principle, doctrine, or standard may supplement, bypass, or override that process.

### Formal

> The jurisdiction to invalidate any instrument under this Article vests exclusively in the mechanism established by Clause 4. A court exercising that power shall derive its authority solely from Clause 4.

### Sovereign

> The jurisdiction to nullify any instrument under this Article subsists as sovereign and indefeasible, vesting exclusively in the mechanism established by Clause 4. A Court exercising that power shall derive its authority solely from Clause 4 and shall brook no doctrine that purports to supplement, circumvent, or supersede that mechanism.

### Archaic

> The jurisdiction to nullify any instrument under this Article subsists as sovereign, indefeasible, and absolute, vesting exclusively in the mechanism established by Clause 4 and in no other power, authority, or body whatsoever. Any judgment rendered in derogation of this exclusive jurisdiction is hereby declared void and of no standing.
