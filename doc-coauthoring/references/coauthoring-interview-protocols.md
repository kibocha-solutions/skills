# Document Co-Authoring Interview Protocols

## 1. Collaborative Context Gathering

Co-authoring replaces passive document generation with interactive discovery. The goal is to surface unstated assumptions, technical constraints, and organizational tradeoffs before drafting text.

## 2. Interview Phases

1. **Orientation**:
   - Classify document category (PRD, RFC, Architecture Spec, Policy, Whitepaper).
   - Identify primary reader persona and their decision threshold.
   - Establish tone and register.

2. **Targeted Inquiry**:
   - Ask five to ten numbered, specific questions focused on boundaries, non-goals, and failure modes.
   - Avoid open-ended queries ("Tell me about your project") in favor of concrete operational choices ("Will data be retained for 30 or 90 days?").

3. **Curated Option Generation**:
   - Offer concrete formulation options for disputed sections.
   - Group options into tradeoffs (e.g., Option A prioritizes throughput; Option B prioritizes auditability).

4. **Independent Reader Testing**:
   - Formulate five questions an outside reader must be able to answer using solely the document.
   - Test against an independent agent or clean context to locate unstated assumptions.
