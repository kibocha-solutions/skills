# Source and Standards Research

## Procedure

1. List every supplied source and governing standard.
2. Read supplied sources in full before modeling them.
3. Separate authoritative requirements from examples, commentary, and proposals.
4. Preserve source terminology.
5. Record each diagram element that depends on a source.
6. Use primary and authoritative sources for current external standards.
7. Record the source title, section, version, date, and link or local path.
8. Map each externally constrained node, edge, label, and boundary to its source.
9. Keep unsupported candidate content outside the delivered diagram.
10. Recheck the final source and export against the source map.

## Source map

| Diagram element | Requirement | Source | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stable element ID | Required representation | File or URL | Section or page | Verified or unresolved |

## Constraints

- Do not invent missing relationships.
- Do not upgrade examples into requirements.
- Do not merge distinct source terms for visual convenience.
- Do not carry stale facts from a prior diagram into a new one.
- Put unresolved source questions in chat or the planning record.
