### Good Example: Standard Format

```text
Subject: Project Application. Refined Technical Proposal and Budget

Dear Director,

This note accompanies the refined technical proposal and budget for the partnership application to the donor call for proposals, due on 7 October 2026 at 23:59 East Africa Time. The attached documents supersede the earlier drafts sent in the course of our correspondence.

We refined the application against the call, the official clarifications issued by the donor, and standard proposal-writing practice. Every change was necessary for compliance or competitiveness. As such, we made the following changes to the deliverables —

1. Statements that weakened the lead organization's eligibility, placeholder text, and repeated content were removed.
2. Every response was brought within its prescribed word limit.
3. The problem statement now rests on verified published demographic and health data.
4. Target groups, selection criteria, and coverage numbers are defined.
5. The workplan aligns with the statutory budget calendar and national election timeline.

The narrative reads as the partnership's own analysis and satisfies donor originality requirements.

The budget changes ensure the application remains competitive and compliant. The adjustments below keep the budget defensible by matching each partner's share of the work to its share of the funds —

1. Lead Partner: US$97,950, representing 56.1 per cent of the total budget. Grant management, regional engagement, budget scorecards, successor costed health plans, monitoring, evaluation, and audit.
2. Implementing Partner: US$54,188, representing 31.0 per cent of the total budget. County programme operations, community scorecards across designated health facilities, public commitment tracking, and national youth engagement.
3. Community Partner: US$22,561, representing 12.9 per cent of the total budget. School listening forums and mentoring in the target counties, subject to institutional school confirmation.

Every figure traces to a costed activity, and the total of US$174,699 is within the US$175,000 ceiling. Audit and visibility costs are allocated under direct services per donor guidelines.

Personnel costs were not reduced, although they carry a non-negligible risk. Staff salaries and benefits constitute 37.6 per cent of the budget. Direct activity costs stand at 53.6 per cent. In comparable reviews, donors have required staff to be charged only for the share of their time spent on the project, and have challenged budgets where operating costs outweigh activity costs.

Recommendations

I recommend that the partnership —

1. Share the attached documents with the lead partner this week so that the consortium can consolidate them before the deadline.
2. Raise direct activity costs to at least 65 per cent of the budget by charging staff only for the verified share of their time spent on the project.
3. Request salary scales and travel rates from the lead partner to finalize figures against audited costs.
4. Obtain written confirmation of operating schools and registration status from the community partner.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Title]
[Organization]
```
